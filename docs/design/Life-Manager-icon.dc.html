Why the shortest bar is amber
It's the same amber the app uses for a date that's approaching. The icon states the app's job — something is coming up — without depending on colour: the bar is also the shortest.
Why no glyph
A folder, a shield or a passport would name the filing cabinet rather than the reason to open it, and all three are crowded categories on a home screen.
Production notes
Bars sit on a 1024 grid at 20/74%/40% width, 66px inset, corner radius 62 at 280 (0.222 of the square). Ship the ink version as primary, paper for light-mode tinting, and a full-bleed square for Android's adaptive mask.
